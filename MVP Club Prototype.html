Before you begin

The founder mindset.

10 things to keep with you every step of the way.

01
Have an open mind
Your first idea will change. That's not failure — that's learning.
02
Be open to criticism and scrutiny
Harsh feedback early saves months of wasted effort.
03
It's a rough world out there
Building is hard. The founders who make it keep going anyway.
04
Don't build before you validate
Talk to real people first. Then — only then — write code.
05
Sell before you spend money
Get a "yes, I'd pay" before investing anything.
06
Fall in love with the problem
Your solution will change. The problem stays constant.
07
Done beats perfect, every time
Ship it rough. Momentum matters more than polish.
08
Talk to strangers, not just friends
Friends are polite. Strangers are honest. You need honesty.
09
This community is here to help you
Ask questions. Share honestly. Lift others when you can.
10
Stay positive — it compounds
Optimism is a strategy. Positive founders move faster.
