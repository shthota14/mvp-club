How to use private messaging
1

Find the inbox icon

Look for the ✉️ envelope in the top-right of every page. A red badge shows how many unread messages you have.

✉️ Always visible in the nav
2

Compose a new message

Click ✏️ Compose in your inbox. Search for any member by name or email. Type your message and hit Send.

Search by name or email
3

Message from a community post

On any idea page, click a member's name or avatar. You can open a direct conversation with them from there.

Great for following up on a post
4

Message the admin

The admin appears in your search results. Message them to report issues, ask questions, or get support on your idea.

Admin usually replies within 24h
🔐

100% private — never shown publicly

Private messages are never shown in the community feed, on idea pages, or anywhere else in the app. Only you and the person you're messaging can see the conversation. Messages are not public and not searchable.

Common questions
📍
Where do I find my inbox?
Click the ✉️ envelope icon in the top-right corner of any page. It's always there, for both regular users and admin.
🔔
How do I know when I have a new message?
A red badge appears on the ✉️ icon with the number of unread messages. It updates automatically every 30 seconds.
✓
Can I tell if someone read my message?
Yes — your sent messages show a ✓ Read indicator once the other person has opened the conversation.
🚫
Can I message any member?
Yes — use Compose and search by name or email. You can message any approved member or the admin. Admins cannot be messaged by anonymous visitors.
