What is a Business
Model Canvas?

A single-page visual that maps out every critical dimension of your business — before you write a single line of code.

🗺

Created by Alexander Osterwalder, the Business Model Canvas (BMC) is a strategic tool that replaces a 30-page business plan with 9 focused building blocks on one page. It forces you to think clearly about how your business creates, delivers, and captures value.

The 9 building blocks
1
🤝 Key Partners
Who do you need to work with to make this happen?
Suppliers, alliances, outsource partners
2
⚙️ Key Activities
What do you do every day to deliver value?
Build, sell, support
3
⭐ Value Proposition
What problem do you solve? Why choose you over alternatives?
Your unique promise to the customer
This is the heart of your canvas. Everything else connects to it.
4
💬 Customer Relationships
How do you get, keep, and grow customers?
Self-serve, personal, community
5
🎯 Customer Segments
Exactly who are you building this for?
Mass market, niche, B2B, B2C
6
🏗 Key Resources
What assets do you absolutely need to operate?
People, tech, IP, cash
7
📣 Channels
How do customers find and receive your product?
Web, app, sales, referral
8
💸 Cost Structure
What does it cost to run this business? Fixed costs, variable costs, biggest expenses?
Infrastructure · Salaries · Marketing · Support
9
💰 Revenue Streams
How do you make money? What are customers actually paying for?
Subscription · One-time · Usage-based · Freemium

The BMC test: can a stranger understand your business in 60 seconds?

If someone picks up your BMC and can't immediately understand what you do, who you serve, how you make money, and why customers would choose you — it's not clear enough yet. Revise until it passes the 60-second test. In MVP Club, your BMC lives inside My Idea Vault and can be updated at any stage.
