Community
& Seeking Help

The community is where founders learn from each other. Here's how to post, comment, react — and ask for exactly what you need.

What you can do in the community
📣

Post an update

Share a milestone, insight, or question about your idea. Posts appear on your idea's public page.

1 Go to Community and open any idea
2 Scroll to the posts section
3 Click Add update and write your post
👀

Browse other ideas

Filter by stage to find founders at the same point as you. Tap any card to see their full post feed.

1 Click Community in the nav
2 Use the stage filter pills to narrow down
3 Click any idea card to open it
💬

Comment on a post

Comments are public and visible to all members. Offer feedback, share resources, or ask questions.

1 Open an idea and find a post
2 Click 💬 Comment or the comment count
3 Type your comment and submit
⚡

React to posts

Two reactions keep things focused: Encourage to show support, Ask How to invite them to share more.

1 Find a post you want to react to
2 Click 👍 Encourage or 🔍 Ask how
3 Reactions are visible to the poster instantly
