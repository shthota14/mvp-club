The 5-Stage
Founder Journey

MVP Club guides you through five clear stages — from raw idea to real business. Here's what each one means and what to do.

💡 Idea
→
🔍 Hone
→
✅ Validate

🚀 Done
💡
Stage 1
Idea
You have something in mind. It might be fuzzy. That's fine. This stage is about getting it out of your head and into words.
✓ Describe your idea in one sentence
✓ Add it to your Idea Vault
✓ Let admin review and approve it
"What is the thing I want to build?"
1
🔍
Stage 2
Hone
Sharpen the idea. Get specific about the problem, the customer, and why your solution is different. Stop being vague.
✓ Define the exact problem you're solving
✓ Identify who specifically has this problem
✓ Draft your Business Model Canvas
"Who has this problem and why do existing solutions fail them?"
2
✅
Stage 3
Validate
Talk to real humans. Get evidence that people actually want this. Your opinion doesn't count — their behaviour does.
✓ Interview at least 5 potential users
✓ Track contacts in your Validation Tracker
✓ Post your key finding to the community
"Will real people pay for, or meaningfully engage with, this?"
3
🛠
Stage 4
Shape
Define exactly what you'll build. Ruthlessly cut scope. Your MVP should do one thing well — not everything poorly.
✓ Write down your 3 core features (max)
✓ Finalise your Business Model Canvas
✓ Share your MVP scope with the community
"What is the smallest thing I can build to prove this works?"
4
🚀
Stage 5
Get It Done
Build, ship, and share. Momentum beats perfection. Even an imperfect launch teaches you more than a perfect plan.
✓ Build your MVP
✓ Post your launch to the community
✓ Celebrate — then iterate
"Have I shipped something a real user can use today?"
5
⚡

The only rule: keep moving

Founders don't fail from lack of information — they fail from lack of movement. MVP Club exists to give you one clear next step at every stage. When you're stuck, go back to the key question for your current stage and take the smallest action that answers it.
