4
Stage 4
💰 Build a Sustainable Business
Measure outcomes not activity
Metric Why it matters Track it when
MRR / ARR Business viability — are you making money? From first paying customer
Retention Product value — do people keep using it? Week 2 after launch
CAC Growth efficiency — how much to acquire a customer? When running paid channels
LTV Profit potential — how much is each customer worth? Once retention is stable
Activation Rate User adoption — are new users getting to value quickly? From launch day
📊
Rule: Track a small set of metrics that influence decisions. Avoid vanity metrics that look good but tell you nothing.

The 10 Founder Operating Principles
01
Customer conversations beat assumptions — always.
02
Solve painful problems first. Interesting problems second.
03
Launch before it feels comfortable. Perfection ships nothing.
04
Learn faster than your competitors. Speed of learning is your moat.
05
Retention comes before growth. Fix the leaky bucket first.
06
Cash is strategic oxygen. Protect it until revenue is predictable.
07
Focus creates momentum. Say no to almost everything.
08
Data informs decisions. Gut instinct proposes; data disposes.
09
Great teams compound results. One A-player beats three B-players.
10
Value creation drives everything. Revenue follows value — not the other way around.
