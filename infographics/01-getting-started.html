Getting Started
with MVP Club

From zero to your first session in under 5 minutes. Here's exactly what to do.

1

Create your account

Visit the app and click Get started on the home screen. Enter your name, email, and a password. That's it — no credit card, no lengthy onboarding.

✓ Takes less than 60 seconds
2

Pick your current stage

MVP Club works around 5 stages. Choose the one that best describes where you are right now. You can change it anytime.

💡 Idea 🔍 Hone ✅ Validate 🛠 Shape 🚀 Done
3

Add your idea

Go to My Idea Vault and click + New idea. Give it a name and a one-sentence description. You can have multiple ideas and switch between them.

5

Wait for approval, then go public

Your idea is reviewed by the MVP Club admin before it appears in the community. You'll see a ⏳ Pending badge until approved. Once live, your idea is visible to all members.

Quick tips
🔒

Your vault is private

Notes, progress, and the Business Model Canvas are only visible to you — not the community.

🔄

Change your stage anytime

Update your stage as you progress. This keeps your community posts and guidance relevant.

💬

Community is public

Your approved idea and its updates are visible to all founders. Comments are open to everyone.

🛡

Admin reviews everything

Content is moderated to keep MVP Club a high-quality, focused space for serious founders.
